## Claims of this release, and their status at sweep time

1. **Degree-≤6 minimality in the ℂ*-equivariant class of weights (1,−1,−2)** — CLEAR.
   arXiv:2607.20210 remains at v2 (Thm 10.10 covers only deg A = deg B = 1; §11 poses the general
   question as open). arXiv:2608.00222 (Gao, announced 4 Aug, read in full) concerns geometric
   degree in dimensions ≥ 4, not equivariant polynomial degree. The dasjoms repository's Note 18
   states the (1,−1,−2) classification "is still unwritten"; jacobianfun.org lists the
   classification under "Not established here."
2. **Moment-map identity ν∘Φ = μ for the cotangent lift** — CLEAR. Two adjacent observations,
   both cited in the paper's Note added: A. Husain (github.com/Cobord/Jacobian, 1 Aug: cotangent
   lift + torus intertwining, no moment map, isotropy posed open) and D. Fillmore (31 Jul,
   comment on Tao's digestion: Majorana spin-addition reading, no symplectic content).
3. **No-go lemma (s-free C, s-affine A and B ⇒ automorphism, all weights)** — CLEAR. No
   approach to this statement found anywhere.
